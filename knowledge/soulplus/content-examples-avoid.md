# SoulPlus AI — Content we do NOT like

> Avoid these patterns. Add real rejected posts here over time.

## Patterns to avoid

- “Your fate is sealed” / doom scrolling mysticism  
- Medical diagnoses or “AI cured my anxiety” style claims  
- Guaranteed wealth, marriage, or health outcomes  
- “Psychic AI knows everything about you” hype  
- Generic wellness fluff with no Destiny Matrix / SoulPlus specifics  
- Fear-FOMO (“If you don’t calculate tonight, you’ll ruin your life”)  
- Wrong brand: Maria’s intimate coaching voice on hard product promo without intent  
- Inventing plan prices or features not on Rates

```
Miss this and stay cursed forever 😱🔥
```

Why it's wrong: Fatalism, overclaim, fear CTA, not our philosophy.
