# SoulPlus AI — Audience

> Status: **ACTIVE STARTER** — refine with research/ads data when available.

## Primary audience

People exploring **self-knowledge, relationships, purpose, and life patterns**, open to Destiny Matrix / numerology / archetypes, and comfortable using a modern AI product (not only traditional astrology apps).

Likely pains:

- Feeling “lost” or stuck in repeating cycles  
- Relationship confusion / compatibility questions  
- Career or life-direction uncertainty  
- Want deeper insight than a generic daily horoscope  

Desires:

- Clarity about strengths and patterns  
- Language for what’s been hard to name  
- Practical guidance + meaningful symbolism  
- Fast, personal answers (calculator + AI chat)  

## Secondary audiences

- Partners / couples exploring Compatibility Matrix  
- Coaches / practitioners who may buy extra transcripts for clients  
- RU- and EN-speaking users (site is EN-forward; confirm channel language mix)

## Jobs to be done

- “Help me understand who I am at a structural/energetic level”  
- “Explain my relationship patterns”  
- “Give me a clear reading I can revisit”  
- “Let me ask follow-up questions in chat”  

## Language preferences

- Product site: English (public)  
- Social / support: match audience; keep brand terms per `terminology.md`  

## Sensitive topics

- Mental health crises → do not position product as therapy; suggest professional help when appropriate  
- Health/finance “forecasts” → guidance/patterns language, not guarantees  
- Human review for aggressive acquisition claims
