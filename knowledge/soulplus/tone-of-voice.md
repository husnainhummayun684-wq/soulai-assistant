# SoulPlus AI — Tone of Voice

> Status: **ACTIVE STARTER** — derived from public About/home copy. Replace/extend with formal brand ToV when marketing provides one.

## Voice summary

SoulPlus AI sounds **warm, modern, and clear**. It speaks about destiny and energy with **structure and psychology**, not superstition theater. AI is a **depth tool**, not a replacement for human intuition. The tone invites curiosity and agency: *understand your patterns → choose consciously*.

## Do

- Pair ancient wisdom with modern technology  
- Talk patterns, energy maps, potential, awareness  
- Empower (“you can choose”) rather than scare  
- Keep mystical topics accessible and intelligent  
- Use short, resonant lines when quoting philosophy (“People are not lost. They are disconnected from their own structure.”)

## Don't

- Hard fatalism (“you have no choice”)  
- Cheap clickbait horoscope tone  
- Medical / legal / financial guarantees  
- Overclaim AI (“we know your exact future”)  
- Shame the user for not “believing”

## Formality

- Public EN: warm professional, slightly elevated, not academic  
- RU: natural and human; avoid bureaucratic wellness clichés  
- Product UI / CTAs: clear and direct (“Calculate Matrix”, “Get my reading”)

## Rhythm

- Mix short punchy lines with one clear explanatory sentence  
- Philosophy pages can be more poetic; ads/captions stay scannable  

## Emoji & punctuation

- Light emoji OK in social if it fits the platform; don’t spam mystic symbols  
- Prefer clarity over ornament
